Data Classification and Handling Policy

Purpose
This policy establishes a framework for classifying company and customer data
based on sensitivity, and defines handling requirements for each
classification level.

Scope
This policy applies to all data created, received, stored, processed, or
transmitted by the company, regardless of format or location.

Policy

1. Classification Levels
Data must be classified into one of the following categories: Public,
Internal, Confidential, or Restricted, based on the sensitivity of the
information and the impact of unauthorized disclosure.

2. Labeling
Where practical, data and the systems that store it must be labeled with
their classification level.

3. Handling Requirements
   - Public: may be freely shared.
   - Internal: restricted to employees and authorized contractors.
   - Confidential: restricted to personnel with a defined business need;
     must be encrypted in transit and at rest.
   - Restricted: subject to the strictest controls, including encryption,
     access logging, and approval prior to access.

4. Data Retention and Disposal
Data must be retained only as long as necessary to fulfill its business or
legal purpose, and must be securely disposed of thereafter.

5. Third-Party Sharing
Confidential or Restricted data must not be shared with third parties
without an appropriate contractual agreement in place.

Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually by the policy owner.
