Acceptable Use Policy

Purpose
This policy defines acceptable use of company-owned systems, networks, and data.

Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties with access to company systems.

Policy
1. Company systems must only be used for authorized business purposes.
2. Users must not attempt to bypass security controls or access data outside their authorization.
3. All software installed on company devices must be approved by IT.
