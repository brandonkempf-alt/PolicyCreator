Vendor Management Policy

Purpose
This policy defines requirements for assessing and monitoring third-party vendors that access company or customer data.

Scope
Applies to all vendors and service providers with access to company systems or data.

Policy
1. Vendors are assessed for security risk prior to onboarding.
2. Vendor access is reviewed on a periodic basis.
3. Vendor contracts include applicable security and confidentiality requirements.
