Acceptable Use Policy

Purpose
This policy defines the acceptable use of company information systems, devices,
and data by all employees, contractors, and third parties.

Scope
This policy applies to all company-owned or company-managed devices, networks,
and systems, as well as any personal devices used to access company resources.

Policy

1. General Use
Employees must use company systems only for authorized business purposes.
Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with job
performance, consume significant resources, or violate any other company
policy.

2. Prohibited Activities
Users must not: install unauthorized software; disable or circumvent security
controls; share credentials; access data outside the scope of their job
duties; or use company systems to engage in illegal activity.

3. Data Handling
All company data must be stored, transmitted, and disposed of in accordance
with the company's data classification standards.

4. Monitoring
The company reserves the right to monitor use of its systems for security and
compliance purposes, consistent with applicable law.

5. Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and
including termination of employment or contract.

Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually by the policy owner.
