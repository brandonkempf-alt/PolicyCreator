Incident Response Policy

Purpose
This policy defines the company's approach to identifying, responding to,
and recovering from information security incidents.

Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and systems involved in
the handling of company or customer data.

Policy

1. Incident Identification
All employees are responsible for reporting suspected security incidents to
the security team as soon as they are identified.

2. Incident Response Team
A designated incident response team is responsible for triaging, containing,
and resolving reported incidents.

3. Classification
Incidents must be classified by severity based on their potential impact to
confidentiality, integrity, or availability of company or customer data.

4. Containment and Eradication
Upon confirmation of an incident, the response team must take action to
contain the incident and eliminate its root cause.

5. Notification
Where required by law, contract, or regulation, affected parties must be
notified of a confirmed incident within the applicable timeframe.

6. Post-Incident Review
Following resolution, a post-incident review must be conducted to document
lessons learned and identify corrective actions.

Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually by the policy owner.
