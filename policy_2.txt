Access Control Policy

Purpose
This policy establishes requirements for granting, reviewing, and revoking access to company systems and data.

Scope
Applies to all systems that store, process, or transmit company or customer data.

Policy
1. Access is granted based on the principle of least privilege.
2. Access reviews are performed on a periodic basis.
3. Access is revoked promptly upon role change or termination.
