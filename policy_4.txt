Data Classification and Handling Policy

Purpose
This policy defines how company data is classified and the handling requirements for each classification level.

Scope
Applies to all data created, received, or maintained by the company.

Policy
1. Data must be classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
2. Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
3. Data handling requirements are enforced regardless of storage location.
