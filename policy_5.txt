Vendor Management Policy

Purpose
This policy defines the requirements for assessing, onboarding, and
monitoring third-party vendors who have access to company or customer data,
or who provide services critical to company operations.

Scope
This policy applies to all vendors, suppliers, and service providers engaged
by the company, including subcontractors with access to company data.

Policy

1. Vendor Risk Assessment
Prior to engagement, vendors must undergo a risk assessment appropriate to
the level of access they will have to company systems or data.

2. Contractual Requirements
Agreements with vendors that process confidential or customer data must
include appropriate security, confidentiality, and data protection terms.

3. Ongoing Monitoring
Vendors with access to critical systems or sensitive data must be reviewed
periodically to confirm continued compliance with contractual and security
requirements.

4. Subprocessors
Vendors must disclose their use of subprocessors that will have access to
company or customer data.

5. Offboarding
Upon termination of a vendor relationship, access to company systems and
data must be revoked and any company data in the vendor's possession must
be returned or securely destroyed.

Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually by the policy owner.
