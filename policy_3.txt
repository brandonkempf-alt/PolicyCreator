Incident Response Policy

Purpose
This policy defines how the organization detects, responds to, and recovers from security incidents.

Scope
Applies to all systems, personnel, and third parties involved in incident detection and response.

Policy
1. Suspected incidents must be reported immediately to the security team.
2. An incident response plan must be maintained and tested at least annually.
3. Post-incident reviews are conducted to identify root cause and corrective actions.
