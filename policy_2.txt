Access Control Policy

Purpose
This policy establishes requirements for granting, reviewing, and revoking
access to company systems and data in order to enforce the principle of
least privilege.

Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and systems that store,
process, or transmit company or customer data.

Policy

1. Access Provisioning
Access to systems and data must be granted based on documented business need
and approved by the relevant system or data owner prior to provisioning.

2. Least Privilege
Users must be granted the minimum level of access required to perform their
job responsibilities.

3. Authentication
All access to production systems must require unique user identification and
multi-factor authentication where supported.

4. Access Reviews
User access to critical systems must be reviewed on a periodic basis to
confirm continued business need; access no longer required must be revoked
promptly.

5. Termination
Access for departing employees and contractors must be revoked no later than
their last day of engagement.

6. Privileged Access
Administrative or privileged access must be limited to personnel whose job
duties require it and must be logged.

Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually by the policy owner.
